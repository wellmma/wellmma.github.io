Wellmma Playground

Parents guide. Kids grow.

Wellmma Playground is an Android app that gives a parent a safe, private learning space for their child. The parent chooses everything the child can see — videos, books, courses, projects, habits and daily plans. There are no ads, no public feeds and no strangers.

Get it on Google Play Read our Privacy Policy

What the app does

Parent-approved videosOnly the videos a parent adds. No recommendations.
Books & coursesReading and lessons chosen by the parent, with progress saved.
Projects & habitsGoals, to-dos and daily plans that build real-world skills.
Parent ZonePIN-protected controls for content, screen time and devices.
Child devicesA child's phone joins with a parent PIN and a 6-digit code. No child account.
Family onlyOptional family chat and AI helper, controlled by the parent.

Your family's data stays with you

Wellmma Playground has no server of its own. Each family's data is stored in a free Firebase project inside the parent's own Google account. We never receive a copy.

Why we ask for Google access

  • Sign in with Google — to sign the parent in on any device. We use the email address and name only for this.
  • Firebase and Google Cloud access — only if the parent chooses Setup Automatically, so the app can create the family's Firebase project in the parent's own account and set it up. We do not touch anything else in the account.

Google access is used only on the parent's device, is never sold or shared, and can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
